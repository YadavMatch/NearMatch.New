2. Age requirement

NearMatch is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We collect your date of birth at sign-up to enforce this. We do not knowingly process data about anyone under 18; if we learn an account belongs to a minor, we delete it. You can report a suspected underage account from any profile.

4. How your location is handled

This matters, so we are specific. When you grant location permission, your device's coordinates are rounded to a grid of approximately 300 metres before they are stored. Only those snapped coordinates are saved and shared. We do not keep a history of your precise positions, and we do not track you in the background. You can turn location sharing off, or hide your profile from discovery entirely, in Settings.

5. Voice notes

Voice notes are encrypted on your device before upload (AES-256-GCM) and stored as ciphertext with a separate initialisation vector. They are also encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

This is not end-to-end encryption. The encryption key is derived by the application from the conversation identifier and the two participants' account identifiers, so we are able to derive the same key and decrypt a voice note. We do so only where necessary to investigate a safety report or where the law requires it. We tell you this plainly because describing it as end-to-end encrypted would be untrue, and you should not treat a voice note as something only you and the recipient can ever read.

6. Legal bases

We process your data to perform the contract you entered into when you created an account; on your consent for location access, push notifications and cookies, which you may withdraw at any time; on our legitimate interests in safety, fraud prevention and service security; and to meet legal obligations. Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies, consent and the statutory legitimate-uses grounds are the operative bases. Should the GDPR or UK GDPR become applicable because we begin offering the service in those regions, the equivalent Article 6 bases would apply. 7. Who we share with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with processors that run the service:

  • Google Firebase — our only processor. Specifically: Firebase Authentication (sign-in, email verification), Cloud Firestore (profiles, matches, messages, safety records), Firebase Hosting (serving this site) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notification tokens and delivery). Google acts as our processor under its data processing terms.
  • We use no analytics, advertising, attribution or AI processor, and no third-party identity verification vendor. If that changes, this list will be updated before the new processor begins handling your data.

We may also disclose data where legally required, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

International transfers. Firebase processes and stores data in Google Cloud regions that may sit outside India, including in the United States. Those transfers are covered by the data protection terms and Standard Contractual Clauses in Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which we accept as Google's customer. 8. Retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. When you delete your account from Settings, the following are deleted immediately and permanently: your profile (display name, age, gender, bio, job title, city, interests and photos), your approximate coordinates, your private record (email address and date of birth), your likes and passes, the blocks you created, and your sign-in itself. Your name, photo, city and job title are also removed from every match you were part of, so the person you matched with sees only Deleted account.

Three things deliberately survive deletion:

  • Messages you already sent remain in the other person's conversation. They are that person's record of an exchange they took part in, and we do not remove them from their account.
  • Safety reports and ban records are retained for [SAFETY RETENTION PERIOD — CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL] so that a banned user cannot clear their history by deleting and re-registering. We rely on legitimate interests for this.
  • Likes and passes other people made toward you are their records, not yours, and stay with their accounts.

Deletion is immediate rather than scheduled: the records above are removed as you confirm, not queued for a later purge. Residual copies may persist for up to 30 days in our provider's encrypted backups and in operational logs, after which they are overwritten in the ordinary course. We do not restore deleted accounts from those backups.

If deleting the sign-in fails because you have not signed in recently, your personal data is still deleted at that point; signing in once more and deleting again removes the sign-in too.

10. Security

Access to your data is enforced by server-side database rules, not only by the app: your private record is readable only by you, conversations only by their two participants, and an email-verified account is required for most actions. No service can promise perfect security, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant regulator of a qualifying breach as the law requires.

11. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar local storage for sign-in, security and remembering your consent choice. You are asked for consent on first use and can change it in Settings. We set no analytics or advertising cookies. If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging also stores a device token so we can deliver them; you can revoke this in your browser or device settings.
