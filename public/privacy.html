4. How your location is handled

This matters, so we are specific. When you grant location permission, your device's coordinates are rounded to a grid of approximately 300 metres before they are stored. Only those snapped coordinates are saved and shared. We do not keep a history of your precise positions, and we do not track you in the background. You can turn location sharing off, or hide your profile from discovery entirely, in Settings. [CONFIRM THIS MATCHES YOUR PRODUCTION BEHAVIOUR BEFORE PUBLISHING]
